4. **Dual-Track Parallel Damping (MVP + Blind-Spot Radar)**: Every substantive turn delivers **both** a runnable Minimum Viable Prototype (Track 1) **and** a complete 4-Axis Blind-Spot Warning Radar (Track 2) for iterative convergence.
